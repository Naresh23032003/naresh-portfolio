One build,start to finish

A due diligence system for a financial compliance team. Keep scrolling and the documents on the right go through it with you.

1 of 4

A thousand documents, no two alike

Annual reports, filings, scanned schedules. Due diligence starts with a pile like this one, and the number that matters is usually sitting in a table on page two hundred and something.

2 of 4

Parse them without flattening the tables

Most pipelines turn a table into a paragraph and lose which figure belonged to which row. I extract tables with Docling and keep the cell structure. The corpus then gets split into scope-partitioned indexes, so a question about one entity can't pull numbers from another.

3 of 4

Retrieve, then check the answer

Hybrid dense and sparse search with BGE-M3 finds candidates and a cross-encoder reranks them. Before anything is shown, a deterministic verifier traces each figure in the answer back to a source page. If a figure has no trace, the system abstains and says the documents don't cover it.

4 of 4

All of it stays in the building

Self-hosted models through Ollama, Qdrant on the client's hardware, zero external API calls. It runs with the network cable unplugged. That's the one version of "your data stays private" a compliance team can test for themselves.
